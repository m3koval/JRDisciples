# The Second Mile / Второе поприще

## Source coverage
All eight pages of the user-provided `Второе_поприще.pdf` were rendered with pdftoppm and read visually; the PDF has no text layer. This is a slide deck, not a full sermon transcript. No missing sermon speech is invented.

1. Title: Второе поприще. From Roman compulsion to radical inner freedom.
2. Matthew 5:41: go two miles when compelled to go one.
3. Roman requisition/carrying illustration and occupation pressure.
4. First-mile mindset: fear, resentment, counting down the minimum.
5. Christ's response: refuse revenge; willingly become a giver.
6. Contrast: compulsion/minimum/resentment versus voluntary love/generosity/peace/witness.
7. Generosity reflects God, whose salvation is not wages. Kind service displays another kingdom.
8. Closing metaphor: freedom begins with the thousand-and-first step.

## Adaptation guardrails
Preserve Matthew 5:41 in the context of Jesus' teaching about responding to enemies. Do not reduce this to productivity or impressing adults. Do not present a precise universal Roman one-mile statute, 1001 metres, or 1000 ordinary steps as established by the verse. No guarantee generosity stops mistreatment or changes another person. No suggestion salvation is earned by extra chores. Jesus saves by grace through faith; service responds to His love. A child's love does not require unsafe obedience, dangerous secrets, or hiding harm. Use a short calm trusted-adult note, not a distracting lecture.

Modern/village cast applications are clearly illustrative, not characters inserted into a biblical account. Every quoted Bible verse must be exact Bible.com ESV/RST, separately from child-friendly explanation.

## Learning arc
See Jesus' surprising teaching → rebuild its words → distinguish revenge/minimum/loving generosity → arrange a practical helping response → receive grace, then serve → choose a real-life second-mile action and discuss it with family.

## Art brief
Six original text-free warm polished 3D animated illustrations. Rounded human children, canonical age/appearance/clothing. Gentle cinematic adventure, honey sunlight, jade/teal landscapes, cream stone and warm wood; vibrant rather than beige. Images teach specific beats. No UI text baked in; no distorted anatomy, threatening violence, fake Scripture, watermarks, logos, money-for-salvation imagery, crowns as merit rewards, or supernatural powers.

- 00-cover: Michael voluntarily shares a basket load with Joseph on a sunlit village footpath; two simple unlettered stone markers and winding path communicate going farther. Inviting composition.
- 01-road: historical context illustration, adult Judean traveler carrying a modest rolled pack beside a Roman soldier on an ancient road. No child forced labor. Unlettered milestone; restrained soldier, no threatening gesture; illustration not proof of a particular law.
- 02-heart: Michael at village cleanup, pausing with a small broom and thoughtful face; his own patch swept, neighboring scattered leaves remain. Joseph in blue nearby carrying a basket. The next generous action is visible, not moral symbols or glowing hearts.
- 03-help: Michael sets an apple basket on a village garden table while Joseph freely offers him water; useful freely chosen help, plausible hands and scale. Original apple-gathering concept was replaced after provider blocks; accepted art and lesson copy use the water-offering scene.
- 04-grace: Rosie in purple with an open Bible and Michael in green listening under an olive tree; warm Scripture light, calm trust, no scales, coins, payment or earning salvation.
- 05-complete: Michael, Joseph, Rosie and Gracie walking together along the path toward their warm village, shared baskets, gentle joy; no trophy for salvation.
