# Junior Disciples Visual Prompt Pack

## Visual lane

Minecraft/Lego-inspired, but public-safe wording:

**Blocky toy-brick / voxel Bible adventure storybook style**

Core traits:
- warm cinematic 3D storybook lighting
- toy-brick / voxel diorama worlds
- open Bible as the physical landscape or story stage
- glowing Scripture light from the pages
- soft rounded kid-friendly characters
- expressive faces and clear emotions
- parent-safe adventure tone: wonder, courage, mystery, rescue; no horror
- chunky blocks, soft moss, lantern glow, golden particles, shallow depth of field
- rich warm oranges/golds balanced with deep blues/purples

Use this wording in generators instead of brand names like Minecraft or Lego.

## Courage Quest: The Cave of Echoes — hero image

Create a wide hero image for a children’s Bible quest called “Courage Quest: The Cave of Echoes.” An open Bible becomes a miniature blocky toy-brick adventure world. A brave child explorer stands at the entrance of a dark but not scary cave, holding a glowing lantern shaped like a small star/cross of light. A gentle lost lamb peeks from inside the cave. The cave walls are chunky voxel stone blocks with soft moss, tiny crystals, and glowing truth lights floating like fireflies. A winding path and sunrise beam in the background show hope and safety. Mood: adventurous, mysterious, courageous, warm, parent-safe. Visual theme: Psalm 56:3, trusting God when afraid. No text, no words, no logos. Wide website banner composition.

## Animation direction for web

Use lightweight web animation first:
- drifting truth-light particles
- lantern glow pulse
- subtle cave shimmer
- slow floating icon movement
- soft parallax if desired
- respect prefers-reduced-motion

Avoid heavy autoplay video until image direction is locked.
