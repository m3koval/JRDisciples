# Source fidelity acceptance — Who am I? / Кто я?

## Required user teaching
- Opening: people's labels (appearance, achievements, grades, opinions) do not determine who we are before God.
- 1 Peter 2:9–10 exact ESV and Synodal text; verse 10 mercy is not lost.
- Chosen: called to belong through Jesus Christ; example being picked last. Do not equate divine election with school-team selection or teach popularity means favor.
- Royal priesthood: through Jesus believers may approach God in prayer, worship, and service. No need to be pastor; not self-mediators or replacement for Jesus.
- Holy: set apart/belonging to God, not claim of never sinning. Stand beside the mocked child, refuse cruelty, seek help when needed.
- God's people: people belonging to Christ, not the building. Care and fellowship rather than solitary identity.
- Witness: proclamation is explicitly about God's mercy and Christ in words as well as deeds, not just generic niceness.
- Two mirrors: people's changing labels contrasted with God's true Word. Scripture, not literal mirror, is authority. Popularity/beauty/skills are not themselves sinful.
- Gift before response: God's mercy through Christ gives belonging; good behavior does not earn it. Church attendance does not automatically save.
- Ending returns to opening: not just WHO am I, but WHOSE am I? No pressure or assurance based on completing activity.

## Product acceptance
- Connected, natural child-facing EN and RU paragraphs and dialogue.
- 15–20 minute teacher-led lesson and concise discussion prompts.
- Booklet: four pages each language; one US Letter sheet front/back folded once; imposed side two rotated 180 degrees for long-edge setup. Reading-order copies separate.
- Harder puzzles without harder language; independent verified solutions, separate answer key.
- Simple clean B&W cartoon coloring; QR top-right links to verified passage while lesson is unpublished.
- Visual QA all reading pages + imposed orientation; no clipped text, internal notes or answer key in student pages.
- Web: bilingual parity, interactive retry/feedback, responsive touch layout, actual illustration, build and browser tests.
- Production publication is not implied by local readiness; exact target must be read back after authorized publish.
