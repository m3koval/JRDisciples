## Sermon structure and timestamps

- `00:51–01:49` — The question of building the Church; Jesus is the model; Matthew 16:18; sermon title.
- `02:08–02:58` — Three road markers: **GROW · BUILD UP · REACH**.
- `03:01–16:54` — **GROW** from Luke 2:40, 52:
  - wisdom / intellectual growth
  - stature / physical growth
  - favor with God / spiritual growth
  - favor with people / social growth
- `05:39–06:32` — Jeremiah’s road-marker illustration (Jeremiah 31:21), applied to the three church-vision words.
- `08:49–09:13` — Knowing much but doing little contrasted with wisdom that applies knowledge.
- `16:54–24:10` — **BUILD UP**, three principles:
  1. Jesus gathered people around Himself and built relationships (`17:24–18:36`; Mark 3:14).
  2. Jesus showed the example in daily life and taught through deed and word (`18:36–20:15`), including washing the disciples’ feet.
  3. Jesus gave responsibility and sent disciples into practical ministry, teaching and correcting them through mistakes (`20:15–22:03`).
- `24:10–31:55` — **REACH**:
  1. many people through public preaching (`24:10–27:02`)
  2. individuals through personal meetings (`27:02–30:28`), including the Samaritan woman, Zacchaeus, and the man across the lake whom Jesus set free
  3. new people through prepared and sent disciples (`30:28–31:55`)
- `31:55–33:28` — Summary and personal questions:
  - What am I growing in?
  - Whom am I building up?
  - Whom am I reaching?
- `33:28–34:54` — Each believer’s responsibility to imitate Jesus; close with the Jan Hus memorial and hope for a church ordered by God’s Word.

## Adaptation decisions

- The lesson preserves the sermon’s title, main Scripture, three-word sequence, subpoints, principal Gospel encounters, foot-washing example, road-marker metaphor, and closing questions.
- Jan Hus’s execution and the former-prisoner building-site story are not dramatized for children ages 6–10. Their theological function is retained through the Scripture-first road-marker theme, repentance/Gospel call, service example, and responsibility questions.
- The Gospel clarification explicitly states that Jesus died for sin and rose again and that growth, service, church attendance, and lesson completion do not earn salvation.
- “Living stone” and Church-membership language is conditioned on trusting and following Jesus.
- Outreach actions require parent, guardian, teacher, or trusted-adult involvement.
