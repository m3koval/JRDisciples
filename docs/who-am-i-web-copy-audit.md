# Who Am I website copy review — unreleased

Base: origin/main 2703ec4. Scope: website lesson, related listing/feature copy, requested homepage compact CTA. Print v18 PDFs and Scripture source untouched.

## Editorial rationale
- Opening: replaced an abrupt reported problem with a child’s action, Rosie listening before advice, and a clear move to Anna’s table. Cover explicitly depicts a later welcome.
- Chosen: Peter addresses believers together, rather than choosing a star performer. Michael’s question connects the passage to his hurt.
- Royal priesthood / holy: separated compressed definitions into questions a child could actually ask. King Jesus, direct prayer through Jesus, worship and service are clear; holy is belonging and learning, not perfection. Prayer follows explanation instead of interrupting it.
- Soccer: last chance to tie, missed ball and fall, game over, blame, disappointment, helping hand and invitation to sit remain explicit. No retry and no sin attributed to the missed kick.
- Church: table transition now motivates Michael’s question. Christ’s family is not a building or sports team; inviting Ben does not automatically convert him.
- Witnesses: Ben recalls what Rosie already said; no reset. Words interpret kindness and point to Jesus. Light/darkness explained without inventing physical props.
- Mercy: Anna introduces the passage’s ending; narrator gives death/resurrection, sin, forgiveness, faith, and response-not-payment. Children do not recite adult doctrinal monologues.
- Recall: explicit verse 9 study followed by exact verse 10 recall. Study remains hidden in practice; return-to-study retained.
- Sidebar: explains the comparison rather than suggesting two unintroduced literal story mirrors.
- Completion: reviewed and retained the later welcome, optional response, honest prayer, no conversion pressure.
- Activities: reviewed every prompt, option, hint, feedback and answer mapping; retained clear effective items. Removed ambiguous matching of helping (also service) by testing holiness directly; hints now invite reasoning. Recall explains deselection.
- Adult notes: corporate identity, grace, access through Christ, pastoral care and safety retained. Updated pacing and clarified shorter print edition.
- UI labels: navigation, progress, hint disclosure, study return, retry, print instructions and privacy reviewed. Functional concise labels retained; error feedback made warmer.
- Listing/homepage: story-led invitation, realistic 20–30-minute estimate, requested yellow hero button now points to Who Am I; prayer feature remains below.
