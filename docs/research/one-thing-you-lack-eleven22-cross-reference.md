# Church of Eleven22 cross-reference — rich young ruler

Source: [Little Child vs Rich Young Ruler — Impossible to Possible — Matthew S5E9](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dx03mEwgHKM)

Reviewed from the English original auto-caption track on 2026-08-16. Captions are an alignment source, not a word-for-word authority; Scripture remains authoritative.

## Gold retained in the child lesson

- **04:07–05:17:** Matthew deliberately contrasts children with nothing to offer and a rich young man who has kept commandments. The passage dismantles the idea that people can be good enough for God.
- **10:14–13:43:** The children come with open hands. The kingdom is received by grace, not climbed into or purchased.
- **24:50–26:10:** Jesus' command reaches the specific ruler of the man's heart. Selling everything is not presented as a universal salvation price.
- **32:23–34:23:** “Who then can be saved?” is the right question; salvation is impossible by human goodness but possible with God.
- **43:33–45:18:** Jesus' love exposes and invites; His love is not cancelled by the man's refusal.

The first item was the only substantial addition needed. The existing lesson already clearly taught the other four truths.

## Child-facing addition

The children had no status or treasure to offer and simply came. The young man came with achievements and full hands. This does not teach that children are sinless; it teaches that God's kingdom is received as Jesus' gift, not earned as a prize.

## Not imported

Adult-specific illustrations, application to marriage/depression, extended cultural material, and speculative rhetoric were left out because they do not improve the child lesson.
