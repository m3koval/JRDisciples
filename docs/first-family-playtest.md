# First family playtest — gameplay and rewards

## Today's scope
One complete, replayable Lost Lamb assignment inside Junior Disciples. Keep the current visual direction, touch walking, jumping, map and contextual interaction. Do not delay the first playtest for a shop, large campaign, additional cast, generated voices or paid assets.

## Playable story spine
1. One lamb left the fold; the others are waiting at home.
2. Follow visible hoofprints along the path. The goal is discovery, not collecting arbitrary points.
3. Tracks cross a creek with a damaged bridge. Find and carry two boards to make a safe crossing.
4. Explore the meadow and listen for the lamb's bell. The map shows a search area, not a spoiler pin.
5. Approach and call gently, then walk home at a pace the companion can follow. Getting ahead is recoverable, not a game-over.
6. The flock is together again. Show what the child accomplished, award the rescue, choose a home banner, and offer a clear finish or optional exploration.

## Reward design contract
Adventure Points are accomplishment records, not money, righteousness, faith, salvation, intelligence or sibling rankings. No random loot, purchases, daily-streak pressure, timer bonuses or penalties for hints/falls.

- First trail discovery: 10.
- Complete bridge: 20.
- Find the lamb: 20.
- Bring the lamb home: 50.
- Optional complete seed garden: 30.

Each milestone awards once per local save. Replays remain available without farming duplicate points. Derive total from valid earned milestone IDs, not an editable arbitrary total. A full main assignment is 100; optional garden brings the chapter maximum to 130. Native tests must assert these totals, migration, replay and corruption handling.

## Something to earn, not just a number
Rescue unlocks a visible home banner with gold/blue/green choice. All colors are equally valid; changing a choice is allowed. Garden discovery produces visible flowers. The already built bridge, returned lamb, home lanterns and chosen banner make consequences visible. No points-shop for this first level; no locking story access behind grind.

## Local progress boundaries
Existing saved rescued/garden achievements must migrate without losing earned cosmetic access. New milestone saves are local to this browser/device, not cloud sync or authenticated child profiles. A reward ledger does not imply mid-mission checkpoints. Multiple children sharing one browser currently share that save; separate profiles/save slots are a later product decision, not an unimplemented promise.

## Future story proposals, NOT implemented levels
- The Dry Garden: notice a need, restore a small water channel, help the village garden.
- The Wind-Torn Shelter: inspect what is damaged and choose sturdy repairs before changing weather.
- The Neighbor's Delivery: plan a route and safely deliver needed supplies.
Each should introduce one meaningful action, retain exploration and reveal a visible consequence. No generic fetch quest multiplication or points presented as spiritual standing.

## Family acceptance, beyond automated checks
The child can find movement controls, understand whom to help, notice clues, discover why repair matters, call/escort the lamb, choose the reward and stop at a satisfying end without adult step-by-step directions. Parent observes confusion and frustration, not just completion time. Real iPad/iPhone Safari performance and actual enjoyment require this playtest.
