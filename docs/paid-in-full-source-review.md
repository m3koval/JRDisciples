# Paid in Full — sermon source and content review

Source: user-supplied `Оплаченный вексель.docx` (original preserved in chat document cache). Full document read, 283 extracted paragraphs/lines. Original heading varies between the unpaid/paid note; child-facing title chosen as Paid in Full / Оплачено полностью, expressing the sermon conclusion.

## Sermon-specific teaching spine

1. 1 Peter 1:17: the same God is the believer's loving Father and impartial Judge. Reverence is serious respect, not panic and not permission to ignore sin.
2. 1 Peter 1:17 and 2:11: believers are travelers; their hope is with God. This does not excuse neglect of family, school, neighbors, or the earth.
3. 1 Peter 1:18–21: redemption is through Christ's precious blood, not silver/gold; God raised him from the dead; our faith and hope are in God.
4. Colossians 2:13–14: forgiveness and the cancelled record of debt. 'Paid in full' is a teaching illustration, not an inscription the passage says appeared on the cross.
5. Respond in repentance, faith, gratitude and obedience. Good deeds are the fruit of grace, not a purchase price for forgiveness. No quiz score certifies salvation.

## Historical checks

- National Park Service, Lincoln's New Salem 1830–1837: https://www.nps.gov/liho/learn/historyculture/newsalem.htm. Full article fetched. Lincoln partnered with William Berry in January 1833, sold his interest in April, carried partnership debt after Berry died in 1835, and ultimately repaid it. Do not imply James Short cancelled all Lincoln's financial debt.
- Surveyors Historical Society, Carl Sandburg excerpt: http://www.surveyhistory.org/lincoln_the_surveyor1.htm. Full article fetched over HTTP; HTTPS broken at source. It distinguishes Bill Green supplying a horse from James Short buying and returning saddle, bridle, compass and surveying instruments. Child retelling should say Short returned tools, not state that he bought every item including the horse. Source's debt figure differs from sermon; omit precise note amount/interest/date rather than harmonizing unverified details.
- Royal Household, Richard I: https://www.royal.uk/richard-i. Full article fetched. Confirms capture in Austria, ransom of 150,000 marks of silver and release in 1194. Omit silver tonnage/crown-revenue conversions not established by this source. This illustrates ransom terminology, not a claim that monetary price measures a person's intrinsic worth or that God paid Satan.

## Adaptation boundaries

- Do not repeat the sermon inference that a child without a father lacks love, care or protection. God knows and welcomes children in all family situations; human parents are not perfect images of God.
- Do not claim God cannot show compassion merely because he cannot be bribed.
- Avoid attributing personal conquest of Israel to Cornelius; Acts calls him a centurion. Teach impartiality without confusing salvation with ethnic standing or good behavior.
- Real-world bonded labor needs independent sourced nuance and age-appropriate handling. It is not required to recreate detailed child slavery in an ages 6–11 interactive lesson; the core redemption metaphor can be taught with ordinary debt and historical rescue illustrations.
- Sin is not identical to borrowing money. A debt note is an analogy, not an account of a literal document created for each baby.
- Depict cross and resurrection without graphic injury. No visual depiction of God the Father. Art is clearly lesson illustration, not historical evidence.
