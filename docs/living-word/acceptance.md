## Theological gates
- Literal resurrection and Christ's life-giving promise retained; no become-angels teaching, universal salvation, or denial of grief.
- Scripture is true; readers remain fallible and corrigible. No immunity to error claim.
- God saves through faith by grace; obedience and spiritual fruit are response, not purchase price.
- Feelings matter but do not establish truth. God's miracles are not denied; spectacular feelings not mandatory proof of salvation.
- No child verdicts about another person's salvation. Mistakes lead to help, correction and another try.
- No adult marriage/remarriage details, military/pacifism weapons examples, or attacks on pastors.
- Invented child scenarios distinguished from Scripture.
- Displayed Scripture exact ESV/RST, source URLs and version labels. Source fixture independently extracted from Bible.com in scripture-verified.json; preserve accents/punctuation, permit clearly labeled contiguous excerpts.
