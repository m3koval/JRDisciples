## Blockers
- Full ESV Scripture blocks are included for Luke 2:1–7 and John 1:14, but the draft’s own Publishing Notes require exact wording/licensing verification before publishing. That unresolved Scripture/publication step blocks route promotion.
- Non-Christian ancient writers are mentioned only generally. That is acceptable for draft teaching, but publication should add simple, age-appropriate citations or carefully narrow the claim so it does not overstate evidence.

## Required Fixes
- Verify the ESV quotations exactly and confirm licensing is permitted for public use, or replace the full Scripture blocks with a clearly labeled public-domain translation such as WEB before promotion.
- Add simple source notes for outside witnesses such as Tacitus, Josephus, Pliny the Younger, or Suetonius, while clearly stating what each source does and does not prove.
- Tighten the site-readiness note so the promoted route does not carry unresolved “verify before publishing” instructions.

## Suggested Improvements
- Define “eyewitness testimony” when it appears in the Think It Through section, since the curriculum flags eyewitness as a hard word needing immediate explanation.
- Add one short child-repeatable summary after the Evidence Trail, such as: “Oh, that means Jesus was known by real people in real places, not invented long afterward.”
- The Abraham Lincoln comparison is strong; consider adding one sentence that historical evidence for ancient people often looks different from modern evidence, so children do not expect photos or modern records.
- Keep the calm tone. The lesson handles doubters respectfully and avoids fear pressure or mockery.

## 1% Better Note
