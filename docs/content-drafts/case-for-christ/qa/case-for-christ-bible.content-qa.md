## Suggested Improvements
- Define **evidence** the first time the lesson leans on that idea, since the curriculum names it as a hard word. A child-clear definition could be: “Evidence means clues and facts that help us know whether something is true.”
- Consider adding one short sentence that distinguishes preservation confidence from having every manuscript copy be identical, so children do not hear “copied carefully” as “no copyist ever made a mistake.” The later manuscript section already helps, but a brief nuance near point 3 would strengthen it.
- In the activity, give a suggested sentence connected to the lesson, such as “Luke carefully checked what eyewitnesses said about Jesus.” That would make the activity even more reusable for teachers.
- Parent/teacher usefulness is strong; it could become excellent by adding a short adult note on how to answer, “Why are there different Bible translations?” if children ask after hearing about manuscripts and ESV.
