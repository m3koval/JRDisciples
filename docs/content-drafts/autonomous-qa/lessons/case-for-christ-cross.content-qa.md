## Non-blocking improvement notes

1. Citation precision: the phrase “have peace with God” is most directly from Romans 5:1, while Romans 5:6-11 emphasizes justification, rescue from wrath, and reconciliation. During promotion, either change that wording to “are reconciled to God” or expand the cited range to Romans 5:1-11.
2. Some concepts may still be abstract for younger children: “justified by Jesus’ blood,” “truly God and truly human,” “God accepted Jesus’ sacrifice,” and “clean record.” The draft is acceptable, but promotion could add one more child-clear sentence where these appear.
3. Visual work should avoid graphic crucifixion imagery, blood, fear-heavy scenes, or depicting the Father as a human figure. Use symbolic cross/light/heart imagery, the justice-and-mercy activity, children discussing an open Bible, and resurrection hope. Keep readable Bible text in the UI, not generated images.
