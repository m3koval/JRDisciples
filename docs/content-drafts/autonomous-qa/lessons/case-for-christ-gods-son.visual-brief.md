# Visual Learning Pack Brief — Is Jesus Really God’s Son?

Gate: PASS

## Goal

Upgrade this lesson from text-first apologetics into a child-facing “Identity Trail” adventure. The visuals should help children see that the Bible speaks about Jesus in real places, with real testimony, and with careful truth: Jesus is the Christ, the eternal Son of God, truly God and truly human.

## Required visual pack

1. Hero: Junior Disciples follow a golden evidence path across an open Bible landscape inspired by Caesarea Philippi.
2. Bible truth visual: the open Bible connects creation-light with a humble first-century village, teaching “the Word was God” and “the Word became flesh” without implying Jesus began at Bethlehem.
3. Artifact/history visual: original reconstruction panel for Caesarea Philippi/Banias cliffs, spring, ancient stone steps, and a manuscript-style codex. UI captions must say this is an original reconstruction unless a verified open-license photo is used.
4. Challenge visual: identity-card sorting activity with blank cards and evidence tokens; labels are rendered in HTML, not inside the generated image.
5. Topic thumbnail: glowing Bible, golden identity badge, and rocky Caesarea Philippi-inspired cliffs.

## Prompt and QA rules

- Use polished 3D animated human children from the Junior Disciples character canon.
- Use toy-brick / block-city style for environments and props only; do not turn children into minifigs.
- No readable text, fake Bible words, logos, watermarks, or warped captions in generated images.
- Do not depict God the Father as a human figure.
- Do not use weak Trinity analogies such as water/ice/steam, egg, hats, or masks.
- Historical images must be public-domain/open-license if real photos are used. AI visuals must be labeled as original reconstruction/artwork.
- Visual QA must check child safety, doctrinal clarity, artifact/historical honesty, character consistency, file existence, and route wiring.
