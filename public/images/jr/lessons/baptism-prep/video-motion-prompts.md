# Baptism Story and Lesson Video Motion Prompts

No baptism-specific video has been generated yet. Use these only after Mike approves the still direction.

## 1. Jesus Baptism story video — 6-8 seconds

Create a gentle, child-safe, polished 3D animated Bible story video from the Jesus Baptism still. Keep the same composition and character designs. Jesus stands humbly in the Jordan River as John baptizes Him. The Junior Disciples children observe respectfully from the riverbank, not interrupting the biblical moment.

Motion arc:
0-2s: Soft river ripples move around Jesus and John. Warm sunlight grows brighter through soft clouds. The children lean in with quiet wonder.
2-5s: A gentle dove-like glow descends from above, clearly symbolic of the Holy Spirit coming like a dove. The light rests peacefully over Jesus. No dramatic lightning or scary effects.
5-8s: The scene settles into reverent joy. The children smile softly, the open Bible glows with warm Scripture light, and the river sparkles gently.

Camera: slow cinematic push-in, no shaking, no fast cuts, no warped zoom. Keep faces and hands stable.

Negative prompt: readable text, captions, logos, watermarks, distorted faces, warped hands, extra limbs, scary tone, dark supernatural effects, lightning storm, horror mood, lip sync, talking mouths, excessive spectacle.

## 2. Baptism Prep lesson video — 6-8 seconds

Create a bright child-friendly baptism prep class animation from the lesson topic still. Keep the Junior Disciples kids gathered around a glowing open Bible near the river. The mood is calm, joyful, and no-pressure.

Motion arc:
0-2s: Bible pages gently flutter and warm Scripture light glows. The kids look attentive and peaceful.
2-5s: Clear water ripples softly beside the Bible; a white dove-like light shape drifts above; blank colorful question cards gently float into place with no readable text.
5-8s: Parents/teacher/church family figures in the background smile and pray supportively. The kids nod with understanding. End on a clean still composition suitable for the lesson page.

Camera: gentle slow push-in with light parallax, no fast cuts, no shaky motion.

Negative prompt: readable text, captions, logos, watermarks, distorted faces/hands, extra limbs, scary tone, magical spell effects, pressure-heavy mood, children being forced or upset, lip sync, talking mouths.
