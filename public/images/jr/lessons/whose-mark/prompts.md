# Whose Mark? — FAL visual brief and prompts

## Lesson
- Route: `/lessons/whose-mark`
- Passage: Matthew 22:15–22
- Big idea: Jesus cannot be trapped. We should meet real civic responsibilities honestly, while remembering that people bear God’s image and our worship, deepest loyalty, and whole lives belong to God. If human authority commands sin, obey God first.
- Child age: 6–10

## Global art direction
Polished warm 3D animated-movie still; expressive, anatomically clean human characters; cinematic Jerusalem architecture; rich royal-blue, warm gold, and terracotta palette; soft Scripture-light; gentle parent-safe adventure; detailed fabric and stone; culturally respectful first-century Jewish Jesus with Middle Eastern features, dark hair and beard, cream robe and blue mantle. Recurring Junior Disciples guide Michael is an 8–10-year-old human boy in a green hoodie, shown as a respectful story-portal observer where included. No readable text, letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermarks, split panels, horror, violence, caricature, plastic minifigures, malformed hands, extra fingers, duplicate people, floating coins, glowing political symbols, flags, or worship of government.

## 00 Hero — The impossible question
Wide 16:9 composition. In the Jerusalem temple courtyard, Jesus stands calm and wise at center holding a small Roman denarius between thumb and forefinger. On one side, two formally dressed questioners lean in with falsely friendly expressions; on the other, ordinary listeners watch. Michael in his green hoodie is near the foreground beside a softly glowing open Bible, looking from the coin to Jesus. A beam of warm golden light crosses Jesus while cooler blue shadows surround the attempted trap. Keep Jesus, coin, and Michael inside a mobile-safe center crop. No readable marks on coin.

## 01 The hidden trap
Cinematic 4:3 scene in the temple colonnade. Two groups of adult questioners whisper and plan behind a stone column, one subtly presenting a denarius, while Jesus is visible in the distance calmly teaching families. Michael notices a delicate rope snare lying harmlessly on the floor as a visual metaphor, but nobody is endangered. The contrast is deception in cool shadow versus Jesus’ truth in warm light. No villains with monstrous faces; no readable text.

## 02 Look at the coin
Cinematic 4:3 close story moment. Jesus’ open hand holds a single Roman denarius with a simple non-readable imperial profile. Children and adults lean in; Michael in green carefully observes from the edge with wonder. Jesus’ expression is patient, discerning, and strong. Stone courtyard, warm afternoon light, blue-and-gold color story. Hands and coin must be anatomically and physically plausible; exactly one coin.

## 03 Faithful in both lanes
Cinematic 4:3 modern child-life scene, visually unified rather than split-screen. Michael in green stands at a sunny neighborhood crossing with three connected responsibilities around him: returning a library book, helping clean a shared park, and opening a glowing Bible to pray. A parent and community helper are nearby, friendly and ordinary. The visual message is honest responsibility to neighbors under God, not two competing worlds. No flags, government seals, uniforms implying worship, readable text, or money focus.

## 04 God’s image on people
Cinematic 4:3 sacred but child-friendly scene. Michael, Joseph in blue with his notebook, Rosie in purple holding a Bible, and Gracie in her yellow hood stand with a diverse circle of children. Warm Scripture-light from an open Bible illuminates each unique face; a subtle reflection in a clean brass mirror hints that people bear God’s image. No image or person represents God directly. Emphasize dignity, compassion, and belonging, with soft clouds and a garden courtyard beyond. No readable text.

## 05 Completion — Open hands, loyal heart
Cinematic 4:3 celebratory scene at sunset. Michael and the Junior Disciples leave the temple-story portal with open hands: one hand helping a neighbor with a dropped basket, the other near a glowing Bible. In the background, Jesus’ teaching scene appears through a warm archway with the denarius small and secondary. Golden path light leads forward, expressing wise action and wholehearted loyalty to God. Joyful, humble, adventurous, no trophy, no readable text.
