# Shaped by God / Божья огранка — lesson artwork

## Purpose

A crop-safe 4:3 lesson card and wide lesson hero showing the sermon’s diamond metaphor and Judah’s changed heart in Genesis 37–44.

## Final prompt

Premium polished warm 3D animated movie still for a Christian children’s Bible lesson, no readable text. Foreground: Michael, a kind expressive human boy age 9 wearing his established green hoodie, stands at a safe bright gem-cutter’s workbench, comparing an ordinary gray rough diamond stone with a beautifully faceted clear diamond catching warm golden Scripture-light. The tools are child-safe demonstration tools behind a protective glass shield, no danger. Background visual storytelling, softly integrated like a glowing Bible-story scene: ancient Egyptian palace, adult Judah in modest historically inspired Hebrew clothing steps protectively in front of younger Benjamin and offers himself to a dignified Egyptian ruler, showing sacrificial brotherly love; Joseph’s identity is not revealed visually. An open Bible is the central warm light source beside Michael. Bright saturated jewel colors, rounded expressive human characters, cinematic depth, gentle parent-safe adventure tone, polished high-detail 3D family-film quality, crop-safe composition for a 4:3 lesson card and wide hero. Theme: God changes a jealous hard heart into a repentant sacrificial heart, like cutting a rough diamond. No words, letters, captions, logos, watermarks, minifigs, toy-brick people, mining danger, volcano, darkness, horror, weapons, shackles, violence, distorted hands, extra fingers, or warped faces.
