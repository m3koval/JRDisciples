# David Sling Challenge v2 — Source Still Prompt

## Purpose

Generate one visual target still for approval before rebuilding the game.

## Prompt

Polished 3D animated Bible adventure game scene, wide side-view gameplay camera, bright saturated colors, warm golden sunlight, blue sky with puffy white clouds, cinematic but child-safe. A young shepherd boy David stands in the left foreground on a playful block-style grassy ledge, rounded expressive human child character, curly brown hair, green tunic, small shepherd pouch, historically inspired but kid-friendly. David is spinning a historically plausible sling overhead, with a dotted curved trajectory guide arcing across the valley. On the right side stands a large armored giant opponent inspired by Goliath, child-safe and not horror, wearing bronze armor, helmet, shield, and spear, imposing but not grotesque, no injury and no gore. Between them is a sunny valley with soft hills, small crowd silhouettes in the distance, toy-like block terrain pieces, warm Scripture-light glow near David's sling, game-ready composition with clear empty space for UI overlays, strong depth, soft shadows, high-detail 3D animated movie still, Junior Disciples style, parent-safe adventure tone, suitable for children ages 6-12.

## Negative prompt

No readable text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no gore, no blood, no injury, no scary horror face, no demonic imagery, no dark supernatural mood, no realistic violence, no slingshot fork, no Angry Birds-style pullback slingshot, no LEGO minifig characters, no flat vector art, no emoji, no distorted hands, no warped faces, no tiny unreadable clutter, no copied game UI.

## Notes

- This is a style/scene approval image, not final gameplay art.
- Main characters should be human child-friendly 3D animated style, while environment props may use block/toy-like shapes.
- Leave images free of readable Bible text; Scripture will be rendered in the app UI using exact ESV/RST quotes.
