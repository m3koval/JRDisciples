# Wise Builder Quest — Storyline

Big idea: A wise disciple builds life on Jesus by hearing and obeying His words.

Core verse: Matthew 7:24 — “Everyone then who hears these words of mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock.”

Supporting verses: Matthew 7:24–27, Luke 6:47–49, James 1:22, Psalm 119:105.

Characters: Michael, Joseph, Rosie, and Gracie.

Scenes:
1. The Sandy Yard — Joseph learns that fast and flashy is not the same as faithful.
2. The Rock Foundation — Rosie helps the friends choose obedience over shortcuts.
3. The Storm Test — the house on rock stands because Jesus’ words are trustworthy.
4. The House That Stands — the friends turn the lesson into one faithful step today.

Final takeaway: I build my life on Jesus when I hear His words and do what He says.
