# Courage Quest Video / Animation Prompts

Use these with Seedance 2.0-style image-to-video or another kid-safe image-to-video model.

## Global motion rule
Warm cinematic children’s Bible adventure. Slow camera moves only. Golden Scripture-light pulses gently. Truth lights twinkle softly. Keep faces stable and kind. No fast cuts, no flashing, no warped hands, no distorted faces, no new text.

## 00 Cover hero loop
Source image: /images/jr/quests/courage-quest/00-cover-courage-quest.png

Prompt:
Slowly push the camera toward the glowing open Bible adventure world. Golden Scripture-light rises from the Bible pages. Truth lights twinkle along the path toward the cave. Mica’s lantern glows and gently pulses. Liora watches with hope. The little wooden lamb is barely visible inside the cave. Puffy clouds drift softly. Keep it joyful, parent-safe, polished animated movie style. No text.

## 01 Cave Entrance
Source image: /images/jr/quests/courage-quest/01-scene-cave-entrance.png

Prompt:
Subtle camera push toward Mica and Liora at the cave entrance. Mica lifts the lantern slightly. The golden light spreads across the rounded stones. Liora’s expression softens with hope. Tiny truth lights float upward. Keep the cave mysterious but safe, not scary. No text.

## 02 Whispering Tunnel
Source image: /images/jr/quests/courage-quest/02-scene-whispering-tunnel.png

Prompt:
Slow side-to-forward drift through the tunnel. Soft blue echo waves ripple gently on the cave walls while the lantern’s golden light pushes them back. Hoofprints shimmer faintly. Crystals sparkle. Keep the echo shapes abstract, harmless, and not face-like. No text.

## 03 Cracked Stone Path
Source image: /images/jr/quests/courage-quest/03-scene-cracked-stone-path.png

Prompt:
Gentle camera follow as Mica takes one careful step across the rounded stone path. The lantern lights only the next stepping stone. Truth lights blink along the safe path. The shadow below stays soft blue and non-threatening. Slow, wise, courageous motion. No text.

## 04 Deep Chamber Rescue
Source image: /images/jr/quests/courage-quest/04-scene-deep-chamber-rescue.png

Prompt:
Slow push toward Mica rescuing the wooden lamb. The lantern brightens warmly. Crystals and moss shimmer gently. The soft shadows recede as golden light fills the chamber. Mica’s face remains stable, focused, brave, and kind. No text.

## 05 Badge / Quest Complete
Source image: /images/jr/quests/courage-quest/05-badge-quest-complete.png

Prompt:
Four golden truth lights swirl slowly into a glowing courage badge above the open Bible. Mica and Liora smile with relief and joy. The rescued wooden lamb glows softly. Gentle confetti-like sparkles, no text, no flashing. Warm celebration, parent-safe animated movie feel.
