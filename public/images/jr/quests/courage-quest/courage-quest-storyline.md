# Courage Quest: The Cave of Echoes

## Big idea
A child-safe interactive Bible adventure about courage: courage is not pretending fear is gone; courage is trusting God and taking the next right step.

## Core verse
Psalm 56:3 — “When I am afraid, I put my trust in you.”

## Supporting verses
- Joshua 1:9 — The Lord is with His people wherever they go.
- Psalm 119:105 — God’s Word is a lamp to our feet and a light to our path.

## Characters
- Michael: a young adventurer learning that Bible courage means trusting God while afraid.
- Rosie: Michael’s little sister, sad because her wooden lamb rolled into the Cave of Echoes.
- Little Lamb: a small wooden toy lamb, the rescue object.
- The Echoes: not monsters; they are fear-lies that must be answered with God’s truth.

## Storyline

### 0. Cover / Quest invitation — “Enter the Bible Quest”
An open Bible becomes a glowing adventure world. The Cave of Echoes waits ahead, but golden Scripture-light marks the path. Michael sees the first truth-light and chooses to begin.

Kid truth: God’s Word lights the way.

### 1. The Cave Entrance — “The lamb is lost”
Rosie cries because her small wooden lamb rolled into the cave. Michael feels afraid. The cave whispers, “Turn back. You are too scared.” Michael prays, lifts his lantern, and takes one careful step.

Choice lesson: courage starts by trusting God, not bragging and not rushing.
Verse: Psalm 56:3.
Truth light: God helps me take the next right step.

### 2. The Whispering Tunnel — “The cave tells a lie”
Inside the cave, black-glass stones sparkle. The echo says, “You are alone.” Michael answers with truth: “God is with me wherever I go.” The lie grows small and the tunnel fills with golden light.

Choice lesson: fear may speak loudly, but God’s truth is stronger.
Verse: Joshua 1:9.
Truth light: God is with me in scary places.

### 3. The Cracked Stone Path — “One step at a time”
Michael reaches cracked stones over a deep shadow. He cannot see the whole way, but the lantern shows the next safe stone. He walks slowly, one wise step at a time.

Choice lesson: faith is not reckless; faith walks by the light God gives.
Verse: Psalm 119:105.
Truth light: God’s Word is a lamp for my path.

### 4. The Deep Chamber — “The lost lamb is found”
At the deepest part of the cave, Michael finds the wooden lamb caught between two stones. The echo says, “You cannot finish.” Michael remembers Psalm 56:3, reaches carefully, rescues the lamb, and turns back toward the light.

Choice lesson: I can keep doing right when I feel afraid because God is with me.
Verse: Psalm 56:3.
Truth light: I can trust God when I am afraid.

### 5. Quest complete — “Courage badge”
Michael returns the lamb to Rosie. The cave is still a cave, but fear did not win. The children stand beside the open Bible as four truth-lights become a glowing courage badge.

Final truth: Courage is trusting God and doing the next right thing, one step at a time.
