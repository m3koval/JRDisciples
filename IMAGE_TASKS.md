>
> **Style guide (apply to ALL images):**
> Children's Bible storybook illustration. Warm, vibrant watercolour with bold
> clean outlines. Friendly, age 6–12. Rich colours — deep blues, warm golds,
> earthy greens. No text overlaid. Portrait orientation 3:4 aspect ratio unless
> noted. No violence, no scary expressions (even lions should look powerful but
> not terrifying for small kids).
